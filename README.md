### Step 3: Shipping Selection

After payment, your items will be processed. Unlike full agents where you wait for items to arrive at a warehouse and then consolidate, Kakobuy-Cn often ships directly after a brief processing period (usually 1-3 days for them to pack and prepare).

This is the point where the "how long does Kakobuy-Cn take to ship" question truly comes into play. You'll usually be presented with several shipping options, each with an estimated delivery time and cost.

*   **Standard Air Mail (e.g., EMS, EUB):** These are generally the slowest but most economical. Expect anywhere from 15-30+ days. I only use these for non-urgent, low-value items where speed isn't a factor.
*   **Special Lines (e.g., specific "Tax-Free" lines):** These are often the sweet spot. They might be slightly more expensive than standard air mail but offer better tracking, faster delivery (typically 10-18 days), and often handle customs clearance more smoothly, reducing the risk of unexpected duties. These are my go-to for most hauls.
*   **Express Shipping (e.g., DHL, FedEx, UPS):** The fastest option, often delivering in 5-10 days. However, they are also the most expensive and carry the highest risk of attracting customs scrutiny and duties. I reserve these for urgent items or very high-value parcels where I need speed and am willing to pay the premium and accept the customs risk.

My advice: for most users, aim for the "Special Lines." They offer the best balance of speed, cost, and reliability. Always consider the total weight and declared value; a higher declared value on express lines can sometimes lead to higher duties.

Once shipped, you'll receive a tracking number. Kakobuy-Cn usually provides a link to a universal tracking site (like 17track.net or parcelsapp.com) where you can monitor your parcel's progress.
